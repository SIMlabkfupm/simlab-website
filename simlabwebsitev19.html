About SIM Lab

Membrane science with a sustainable interface.

SIM Lab, Sustainable Interfaces and Membranes, is based in the Materials Science and Engineering Department at King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals. We study how polymer chemistry, surface structure, and processing conditions can be combined to create high performance membranes.

Our work brings together fundamental membrane science and application driven research in desalination, wastewater treatment, and advanced separations.

What we work with

Phase inversion, aqueous phase separation, polyelectrolyte complex membranes, thin film composite membranes, thin film nanocomposites, interfacial polymerization, surface modification, and membrane characterization.

Research directions

From material design to water treatment.

Our research connects molecular design, membrane fabrication, characterization, and separation performance.

Aqueous phase separation

Green routes to polyelectrolyte complex and biocatalytic membranes.

TFC and TFN membranes

Thin selective films for desalination and water vapor separation.

Surface and interface science

Interfacial polymerization, multilayers, fouling, and stability.

Wastewater treatment

Membrane solutions for oily wastewater, produced water, and emerging contaminants.

Advanced materials

Polymer, ceramic, nanocomposite, and functional membrane materials.

Scale up and performance

From laboratory assemblies to practical process conditions.
